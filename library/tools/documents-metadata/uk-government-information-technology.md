# UK Government ICT Strategy Resources

> An official gov.uk collection of UK Government ICT strategy and standards documents — background reference material, not an investigative lookup tool.

## When to use
You need authoritative context on UK public-sector information-technology policy — strategy papers, standards, and guidance an official body actually published. It is useful for understanding how a UK department procures or governs IT (e.g. when a case touches a government supplier or system), but it returns no personal selectors and is not a search tool for people.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/ict-strategy-resources.
2. Browse the linked documents in the collection (strategy papers, standards, guidance).
3. Open a document for the authoritative policy text; note publishing department and date.
4. Pivot: named departments/programmes feed procurement- and org-lookups; for anything person-specific, use a dedicated OSINT tool instead.
