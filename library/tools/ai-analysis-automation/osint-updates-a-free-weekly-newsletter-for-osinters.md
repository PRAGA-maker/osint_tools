# OSINT Updates - a free weekly newsletter for OSINTers

> A free weekly Substack digest that rounds up new OSINT tools, technique changes, and relevant news — a way to keep your toolkit from going stale, not a lookup service.

## When to use
Not for a specific case — for keeping your methods current. Subscribe when you want a low-effort weekly signal on which tools appeared or died, which platforms changed their access, and what investigators are talking about. Useful precisely because OSINT tools churn constantly and a library like this one degrades if you don't track that churn.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Visit the site (hosted on Substack).
2. Enter an email to subscribe — ideally a dedicated research inbox, not your personal one.
3. Read the weekly issue for new tools, method notes, and news.
4. When a new tool or technique is mentioned, verify it independently before relying on it, and add anything durable to your own reference set.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** none (a subscription, not a query)
- **Out:** a weekly curated digest of OSINT tools/techniques/news
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** N/A — it's a periodical; a slow week just means a shorter issue, not a failed lookup.
