# Toddington Online Research Cheat Sheets

> Free reference PDFs from an OSINT training firm — search-operator syntax and social-media recon technique guides to make your *other* tools sharper.

## When to use
You're not sure how to phrase an advanced search, or you want a checklist of angles for investigating a given platform. These are technique/reference sheets — how to build Google dorks, which fields a platform exposes, what to check on a profile. Reach for them to improve your queries, not to look up a specific selector.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open https://www.toddington.com/resources/cheat-sheets/.
2. Pick the relevant sheet (search operators, social-media investigation, etc.) and download the PDF.
3. Apply the syntax/checklist to the actual search engine or platform you're working in.
4. Keep them handy as a query-building reference across investigations.
5. Pivot: the improved query runs on Google, a social platform, or another tool in this library — the cheat sheet just makes that query better.
