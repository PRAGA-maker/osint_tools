# YourDictionary Online Links

> YourDictionary's gateway to foreign-language dictionaries: definitions in English plus a curated set of external dictionaries across dozens of languages.

## When to use
You've hit a word, name, or phrase — in English or another language — whose meaning matters to how you read the evidence, and you want either a solid English definition or a pointer to a reputable dictionary in the relevant language. YourDictionary provides English definitions and word tools, and its foreign-language page links out to dictionaries for many languages, making it a quick jumping-off point when a term is non-English.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open https://www.yourdictionary.com/ for English definitions, or the foreign-language dictionaries page (https://www.yourdictionary.com/articles/foreign-language-dicts) for other languages.
2. For English, search the term directly for definition, part of speech, and usage.
3. For another language, follow the curated link to a dictionary in that language and look the term up there.
4. Use it to disambiguate slang, technical terms, or the meaning of a name/word appearing in source material.
5. Pivot: a decoded term sharpens keyword searches; a name's meaning can hint at origin/language for further targeting.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** a word/term to define or translate (free-text, no selector)
- **Out:** English definitions and links to foreign-language dictionaries — interpretation, not person data
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** no definition for a proper noun, brand, or invented handle — those aren't dictionary words; use a general web search instead.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- A reference/definition resource, not a people-search tool — value is comprehension, not identifiers.
- The foreign-language page is a link directory; the actual lookup happens on the external dictionary it points to.
- Passive: no subject interaction.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Complements `[[alphadictionary-com]]` and `[[synonyms-net]]` — YourDictionary is strong for straight definitions and foreign-language dictionary links, AlphaDictionary for English slang/idiom, Synonyms for expansion; reach for whichever the term needs.

## Trust & verifiability
`trust: trusted` — a long-established mainstream dictionary site; reliable for definitions, and the external dictionaries it links are reputable references you can verify against.
