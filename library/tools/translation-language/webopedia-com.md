# Webopedia (text/chat abbreviations)

> Webopedia's large glossary of SMS/chat/internet abbreviations — a quick decoder for slang and acronyms in collected messages.

## When to use
While reviewing chat logs, texts, forum posts or social messages you hit an abbreviation or acronym you don't recognise (beyond the obvious "LOL"), and you need its meaning to interpret the conversation. Webopedia's text-abbreviations reference expands common (and many obscure) ones. It's a support/analysis resource; it produces no OSINT selectors.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open the Webopedia text-message abbreviations page.
2. Find the abbreviation alphabetically, or search the term within the page/site.
3. Read the expansion and note any context-dependent meanings.
4. Re-read the message with the decoded meaning; watch for coded/evasive usage.
5. Pivot: correctly understood slang can change how you interpret intent, relationships or plans in the messages.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** an abbreviation/acronym (no OSINT selector)
- **Out:** the plain-English expansion(s)
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** an abbreviation not listed (niche, regional, or community-specific) — try a general search or a slang-specific dictionary.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- Human-in-the-loop: none.
- OpSec: passive; look up single terms, don't paste whole private conversations into a third-party page.
- Slang is context- and community-dependent; a listed meaning may not be the intended one — corroborate from surrounding context.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Complements `[[definitions-net]]` (general term meanings) and translation tools — Webopedia for texting/internet slang specifically, those for standard vocabulary and other languages.

## Trust & verifiability
`trust: community` — an established but informal glossary; treat expansions as likely meanings and confirm against context for anything you'll act on.
