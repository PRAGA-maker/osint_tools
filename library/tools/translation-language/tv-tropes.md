# TV Tropes

> A vast crowd-written wiki cataloguing narrative devices, memes, and pop-culture references — a decoder ring for the cultural allusions that turn up in usernames, bios, and posts.

## When to use
You've hit a reference you don't understand in a subject's content — a meme, a fandom in-joke, a nickname or handle that seems to allude to a character or trope — and want it explained. TV Tropes provides the cultural context that can reveal why someone chose a handle, what community they belong to, or what a coded phrase means. It's context/reference, not a person-lookup, so relevance is indirect.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open https://tvtropes.org.
2. Search the trope name, character, work, or phrase you're trying to decode.
3. Read the article for the meaning, origin, and the fandoms/works it's associated with.
4. Apply the context: a trope-derived username may point to a specific fandom/community where the subject is active.
5. Pivot: use the identified fandom/reference to search the relevant communities and platforms for the subject's handle.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** none as a selector (a reference/term you want explained)
- **Out:** none (cultural context and origins, not subject data)
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** no article for the term — meaning it's too niche/local for the wiki; try a general search engine or the specific fandom's own wiki.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- Crowd-edited: explanations are informative but not authoritative — corroborate anything you'll rely on.
- It decodes culture, not people; never a source of PII or records.
- Deep rabbit-holes — stay focused on the specific reference you're decoding.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Complements username/handle-search tools — TV Tropes explains *why* a handle was chosen (the fandom/reference), those tools find *where else* it's used.

## Trust & verifiability
`trust: unverified` — a community wiki; valuable for context, but verify any load-bearing interpretation against the primary work or fandom source.
