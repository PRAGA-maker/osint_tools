# Search Engine Watch

> An SEO/search-industry news site — of value to an investigator as *background learning* on how search engines work and how to query them better, not as a tool that looks anything up.

## When to use
When you want to improve the search skills your investigations depend on: understanding how ranking, indexing, and advanced operators behave, and staying current on changes to Google and other engines. It's a learning/reference resource, not a lookup — reach for it to get better at searching, not to search for a subject.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Go to http://searchenginewatch.com and browse or search articles on search-engine techniques, operators, and platform changes.
2. Read pieces relevant to your workflow (advanced search, indexing quirks, new engine features).
3. Apply the techniques in your actual OSINT searches on other tools.
4. Occasionally, a `name` search here surfaces an author/contributor's `social-profile` — a minor secondary use.
5. Pivot: none directly toward a subject; the payoff is better querying elsewhere.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** a topic/technique you want to learn (or, minor, an author `name`)
- **Out:** educational articles improving your search craft; occasionally a contributor `social-profile`
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** N/A for people search — it doesn't look up subjects; a topic with no article just means it isn't covered.
