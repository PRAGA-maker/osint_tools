# Fake Company Name Generator

> One tool in FakeInfo.net's fake-identity toolkit — it spins up plausible invented company names to give an investigator sock-puppet a believable employer/cover story.

## When to use
You are building an investigator cover identity or sock-puppet persona and need a fictitious employer or business name that reads as real but belongs to no one. Rather than inventing (and accidentally naming a real firm), generate candidates here, then vet them. This produces no subject data — it is OpSec support for *your* legend, alongside FakeInfo's other generators (names, faces, profiles).

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open https://fakeinfo.net/company-name-generator and generate one or more company names.
2. Pick a candidate that fits your persona's industry and locale.
3. **Vet it:** search the name in a real business registry and web search to make sure it does not collide with an actual company (using a real firm's name is impersonation).
4. Pivot: combine with FakeInfo's `[[random-face-generator]]` and profile generators to round out a consistent cover identity.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** none (click to generate)
- **Out:** invented company name(s) — placeholder data, not tied to any real entity
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** N/A — it always returns generated names; the real failure is generating one that matches a real business, so always check.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- Human-in-the-loop: none for generation; a manual real-world collision check is essential.
- OpSec: passive — nothing about a subject is involved; the discipline is on *use* — build your own legend, never impersonate a real company or person.
- Legal/ethical: cover identities must stay within your engagement's authorisation and local law.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Pairs with `[[random-face-generator]]` and profile generators because a convincing sock-puppet needs a coherent set — name, face, employer, and social presence that agree with each other.
