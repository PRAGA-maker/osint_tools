# Random Face Generator

> Generates an AI face of a person who does not exist — a safer sock-puppet avatar than a stolen photo, because it won't reverse-image-match a real human.

## When to use
You are building a sock-puppet account and need a profile picture that (a) isn't a real person you'd be impersonating and (b) won't reverse-image-search back to someone's Facebook. A GAN-generated `face` gives you an original-looking avatar. This is purely persona/OpSec tooling with no lookup value, so direct missing-persons relevance is low.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open https://fakeinfo.net/random-face-generator.
2. Generate a face; refresh until you get one matching the persona's apparent age/appearance.
3. Download the `image` and, before using it, sanity-check for AI artifacts (mismatched earrings, warped teeth/hair, garbled background text) and crop/edit them out.
4. Reverse-image-search your chosen face yourself first — regenerated GAN faces occasionally recur; discard any that already appear elsewhere.
5. Pair with the sibling generators to build a coherent cover identity.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** — (no target input)
- **Out:** a synthetic `face`/`image`
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** n/a — it always returns a face; the "failure" mode is an image with obvious AI tells that would out it as fake.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- GAN faces have recognizable artifacts and a characteristic centered-eyes composition; a trained observer can flag them as synthetic. Inspect and edit before use.
- The same face can be generated by others, so it's not guaranteed unique — reverse-search before committing it to a persona.
- **Never** use a synthetic face to impersonate or defame a real, identifiable individual.
