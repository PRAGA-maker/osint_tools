# 9 Steps to Security in the Digital Age

> A free, plain-language online-privacy guide — a quick OPSEC refresher for keeping your own investigator identity separated and protected.

## When to use
Not for a case lookup — for your own operational security. Skim it when setting up or reviewing your research identity: password/2FA hygiene, VPN use, minimizing tracking, and account separation. Sound OPSEC is the precondition for doing OSINT without leaking your identity back to subjects, and this is an accessible primer on it.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open the guide and read through its steps.
2. Map each recommendation onto your own setup — dedicated research browser, VPN, unique passwords + 2FA, tracker blocking.
