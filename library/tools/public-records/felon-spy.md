# Felon Spy

> A documented **hoax**: it pretends to map "criminals in your neighborhood" from an address, but the data is randomly generated and fake. Included here solely as a warning — do not use it.

## When to use
Never. This entry exists to stop an investigator wasting time on a site that looks like a criminal-records tool but returns fabricated results. If you need real criminal/arrest/inmate data, go straight to official registries and legitimate aggregators instead.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Don't. If you land on https://www.felonspy.com during research, recognise it and move on.
2. Understand the tell-tales: Snopes (2008) and MSNBC confirmed the "results" are randomly generated; its own terms of service contain absurd joke clauses (e.g. users must be 96 years old, changes made on "full moons" / "Mongolian new year").
3. Pivot immediately to real sources: official state/county inmate and court record systems, the Bureau of Prisons locator, and vetted aggregators via `[[search-systems-criminal-records]]`.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** `name`/address (ignored in any meaningful sense)
- **Out:** nothing usable — randomly generated fake "felon" map pins
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** every result is fake, so there is no valid positive or negative — treat 100% of output as noise.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- The entire premise is fabricated; any "match" is random and must never be reported as a finding.
- Do not cite, screenshot-as-evidence, or act on anything from this site.
- OpSec: passive to read, but pointless — there is nothing real to retrieve.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Replace it entirely with `[[search-systems-criminal-records]]` and official government inmate/court locators, which return real records.

## Trust & verifiability
`trust: unverified` / `status: down` — not merely unverified but affirmatively debunked as a hoax by Snopes and MSNBC. There is no scenario in which its output should be trusted.
