# Comparitech Deep-Web Research Guide

> A reading guide, not a tool — it catalogues the deep-web/academic databases worth searching when surface engines fall short, so you know *where* to look next.

## When to use
You need scholarly, legal, medical, or archival material that Google won't surface, and you want a curated list of the right databases to consult rather than guessing. This article groups deep-web resources by domain — general (JSTOR, Archive.org, Library of Congress, National Archives), books (Google Books, Project Gutenberg), law (Law Library of Congress, LexisNexis), medical (PubMed, Science.gov), and academic (arXiv, CORE, IEEE Xplore, NASA ADS) — plus tips like the Unpaywall extension for free copies of paywalled papers. Use it as a menu, then go search those sources.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open the article and skim to the category matching your need (general / books / law / medical / academic).
2. Pick the databases that fit your subject and open them directly.
3. Note the Unpaywall tip for recovering free versions of paywalled results.
4. Run your actual `name`/topic queries in those databases.
5. Pivot: for people-focused academic search, jump straight to `[[semantic-scholar]]` and `[[deepdyve]]`, which this library covers as authored tools.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** (none — it is reading material, not a query interface)

- **Empty/negative result looks like:** the guide doesn't list a source for your niche — fall back to this library's index and domain-specific databases.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- It is a **guide**, not a search tool — it produces no data itself; the value is entirely in the databases it names, several of which are already authored here.
- Article content can date; verify each linked database is still live.
- OpSec: passive reading; nothing is disclosed.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Complements `[[semantic-scholar]]` and `[[deepdyve]]` — use the guide to remember the breadth of academic/deep-web sources, then use those authored tools for the actual people-and-publications searching.
