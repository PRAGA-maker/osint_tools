# 192 (UK)

> The UK counterpart to a US people-search: name a person and get their edited-electoral-roll listing, likely household members, directorships, and phone/address history.

## When to use
You have a UK `name` (optionally narrowed by town, `address`, or `phone`) and want to place them: current/prior addresses, an age band, who else is on the electoral roll at that address (`associate`/household), and any company directorships. One of the strongest free-to-start UK tools for building an address history and family/household picture in a missing-persons or locate trace.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Go to http://www.192.com and choose People (or Businesses / Directors / Phone) search.
2. Enter the `name`; add a town/postcode to disambiguate common names.
3. Read the free preview: matched names, approximate location, age band, and number of people in the household.
4. To see full electoral-roll detail / co-occupants, register and spend credits (use a puppet account).
5. Pivot: household co-occupants feed relationship mapping; directorships feed `[[companycheck-co-uk]]`; addresses feed mapping/records tools.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** UK `name` (+ optional town/postcode), `address`, or `phone`
- **Out:** `address` history, `phone`, age band, household co-occupants (`associate`), directorships
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** no match or only a name+area with detail locked — the person opted out of the edited register, is young/recently moved, or you need credits to see more. Absence ≠ non-existence (full register isn't shown).

## Gotchas & OpSec
- Human-in-the-loop: **payment-wall-partial** — previews are free; the useful detail needs credits and an account.
- OpSec: **passive** to the target; your account/payment is a trail — sock-puppet it.
- Only the **edited** electoral roll appears; opt-outs are invisible here. Data can lag reality — verify addresses.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Pairs with `[[companycheck-co-uk]]` (UK directorships) and `[[geneanet-org]]` (family history) — 192 gives the current household/address picture; the others add corporate and ancestral depth.

## Trust & verifiability
`trust: community` — a reputable, long-running UK aggregator, but limited to opt-in/edited data and prone to staleness. Confirm addresses and household links against a second source.
