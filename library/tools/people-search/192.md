# 192.com

> The UK's best-known people/address directory — electoral roll, current and previous occupants, household co-residents and business listings, searchable by name or address.

## When to use
You have a UK `name` and/or `address` and want to place the person: which address(es) they are/were on the electoral roll at, who else lives (or lived) at the household, previous addresses, age band, and any linked business/director records. It is a core UK-jurisdiction people-search source, strongest when you can pair a name with a town or partial address to cut down common-name noise.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Go to https://www.192.com/ and choose **People**, **Address**, or **Business** search.
2. Enter `name` (add a town/area) or an `address` to list occupants.
3. Read the free results: names, age bands, partial addresses and how many people match at a location.
4. For full address, historical electoral-roll years, or a co-resident report, buy credits (the paywall gates the detail, not the existence of a match).
5. Pivot: co-resident `associate` names and previous `address`es feed further searches; confirmed addresses cross-check against property registers like `[[scottishepcregister-org-uk]]` (Scotland) or England & Wales equivalents.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** `name` and/or `address` (UK)
- **Out:** `address` history, household `associate`/co-resident links, `name` matches and age bands
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** "no results" or only distant matches — the person may have opted out of the edited register, be too young/recent, or not be UK-resident; absence is not proof.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- Data comes from the **edited** electoral register plus other sources — people who opted out won't appear, so a miss is common and not conclusive.
- Records lag reality (people move); treat addresses as leads with a date, not current truth.
- Human-in-the-loop: the useful detail is behind a **paywall** — free search confirms a match exists but masks full specifics.
- OpSec: passive toward the target; your purchases tie to your account, so use a sock puppet.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Pairs with property registers such as `[[scottishepcregister-org-uk]]` and with electoral/company sources — 192 gives people↔address links; property registers confirm the dwelling; company records confirm business ties. Combine to triangulate.

## Trust & verifiability
`trust: community` — an established aggregator, reliable enough for leads but not authoritative; opt-outs, staleness and common-name collisions mean every hit should be corroborated before you rely on it.
