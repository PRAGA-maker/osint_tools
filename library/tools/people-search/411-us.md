# 411.com (US)

> A US white-pages people-search run by Whitepages — turn a name, phone or address into addresses, phone numbers, age and likely relatives/associates.

## When to use
You have a US `name` (best with a city/state), a `phone`, or an `address`, and want contact details and household context. It resolves people to current/previous addresses, landline/mobile numbers, age range, and lists of likely relatives and associates — a core US-jurisdiction starting point that pairs a name with a place and a network of associates.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Go to https://www.411.com/ and choose People, Reverse Phone, or Address search.
2. Enter the `name` + location (or a `phone`/`address`); review the free result list.
3. Free tier shows partial data (city, age, some contacts) and how many people match; premium unlocks full reports.
4. Use the age and relatives to disambiguate common names before paying for detail.
5. Pivot: relative/`associate` names and previous `address`es feed further searches; cross-check with `[[searchbug]]` and `[[reverse-phone-lookup-2]]` since aggregators differ.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** `name`, `phone`, or `address` (US)
- **Out:** `address` history, `phone` numbers, and `associate`/relative links (plus age band)
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** no match or only distant matches — the person may be young, recently moved, unlisted, or non-US; absence is not proof.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- Data is aggregated public records — stale addresses and same-name collisions are common; corroborate before attributing.
- Human-in-the-loop: the useful detail is behind a **paywall**; free search confirms a match exists.
- OpSec: passive toward the target; purchases log to your account, so use a sock puppet.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Pairs with `[[searchbug]]` and `[[reverse-phone-lookup-2]]` — different aggregators surface different records; run several and reconcile.

## Trust & verifiability
`trust: community` — a mainstream Whitepages product, reliable for leads but not authoritative; verify addresses/relationships against a second source.
