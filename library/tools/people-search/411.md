# 411

> A US white-pages directory (Whitepages family) for people, reverse-phone, and business lookups — free basics, paywalled detail.

## When to use
You have a US subject's `name`, a `phone` number, or an `address` and want white-pages identification: a name→address/phone match, a reverse-phone owner, likely relatives, or a business listing. It's a standard first-pass directory, closely related to Whitepages.

## How to use it (`bestInteractionPattern`: web-manual)
1. Open http://411.com and choose People, Reverse Phone, Reverse Address, or Business.
2. Enter the selector (`name` + city, `phone`, or `address`).
3. Read the free results — name, city/state, partial phone, and possible relatives; full details prompt a paid report.
4. Note the free data and pivot rather than reflexively paying.
5. Pivot: cross-check on `[[white-pages]]` and `[[allareacodes]]`; feed phones into `[[thatsthem-phone-search]]`.

## Inputs → Outputs
- **In:** `name` (+ city), `phone`, or `address`
- **Out:** listed `name`, `address`, `phone`, likely relatives, business listings
- **Empty/negative result looks like:** no match or only a teaser with everything paywalled — common; absence isn't proof, and mobiles often don't resolve for free.

## Gotchas & OpSec
- Much of the useful detail is paywalled; the free tier is a teaser.
- Data can be stale and skews to listed landlines; corroborate addresses.
- OpSec: passive; the subject isn't notified. Use a sock puppet.

## Overlaps ("do both")
- Overlaps with `[[white-pages]]` (same data family) and `[[allareacodes]]`; run the query across them and reconcile, then confirm phones via `[[thatsthem-phone-search]]`.

## Trust & verifiability
`trust: community` — an aggregated white-pages directory, good for leads but not authoritative and partly paywalled. Verify specifics against a second source.
